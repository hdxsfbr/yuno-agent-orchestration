internal/tools: the registry of platform tools with JSON schemas, risk classes, timeouts and retry budgets; the executor that retries transient failures and runs side effects at most once per idempotency key (side_effects rows, replay marker); the policy that decides auto, require_approval or blocked per agent; and the tools themselves. OpenClaw built-ins (exec, read, write, edit, ls, process, apply_patch) are listed for the UI but executed by the gateway in the sandbox.
