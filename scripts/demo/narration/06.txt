Failures are handled by the platform, not left to the model's good will. First, a limit. I set the Coder's maximum steps to two and start a task that needs more. After the second model call the engine aborts the turn and the run fails with the reason spelled out: step limit two exceeded. The Runs list shows it, and the trace shows exactly where it stopped. The limit is restored afterwards. Second, a rejection. A pipeline run reaches its Deployer, which asks to open a merge request; that call waits for a human. I approve the gate, then reject the merge request, with a reason. The rejection is not an error thrown at the agent: it comes back as a result, with the reason, so the Deployer reports the status and finishes cleanly. No merge request was opened, and the trace records the decision and who made it.
