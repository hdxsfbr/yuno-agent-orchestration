Failures are handled by the platform, not left to the model's good will. First, a limit. I set the Coder's maximum steps to two and start a task that needs more. After the second model call the engine aborts the turn and the run fails with the reason spelled out: step limit two exceeded. The Runs list shows it, and the trace shows exactly where it stopped. The limit is restored afterwards. Second, a rejection. I ask the Deployer to publish a release; it calls the deploy tool, which waits for a human. This time I reject it, with a reason. The rejection is not an error thrown at the agent: it comes back as a result, with the reason, so the Deployer reports the status and finishes cleanly. Nothing was deployed, and the trace records the decision and who made it.
