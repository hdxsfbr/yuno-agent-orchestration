The agent on Telegram is the Chief of Staff, the one that coordinates the others. The Channels page shows the bot, the allowlisted user, and the agent bound to the channel; the channel stays closed until an agent is bound, and only allowlisted users can talk to it. Inbound messages arrive over a verified webhook on the instance's public address. From my phone I ask how the review panel run went, naming the run, and what each reviewer found. The message appears here, and the Chief answers by looking the run up: it reads the run's status together with its three branches, and reports what the security, performance and maintainability reviewers each said, and what the lead decided. The whole conversation is mirrored here, live, and the history is persisted, so it survives a restart and can be inspected afterwards.
