One agent is reachable from Telegram. The Channels page shows the bot, the allowlisted user, and the agent bound to the channel, the Coder; the channel stays closed until an agent is bound, and only allowlisted users can talk to it. Inbound messages arrive over a verified webhook through a public tunnel that exposes nothing else. I send a message from my phone. It reaches the Coder, which answers with its configured personality and tools, and the whole conversation is mirrored here, live, with the reply a few seconds later. The history is persisted, so it survives a restart and can be inspected afterwards.
